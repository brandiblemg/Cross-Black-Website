Request Testing

Since 1989

Independent battery testing in Redford.

Cross & Black was founded in Redford to give OEMs and suppliers an independent place to test automotive batteries. That remains the work.

Purpose

Clear results. No manufacturing interest.

We exist so a quality engineer can send a battery, name a method, and receive a report that does not belong to a factory. The laboratory does not make cells. It does not sell a brand of battery.

The work started with lead-acid. It now includes 12V lithium-ion. The instruction is the same: run the test as written and say what happened.

Leadership

Mike Kaler

Principal Engineer and Vice President · since 1989

Mike has led laboratory operations for more than three decades. His work covers electrical test systems, quality procedures, and OEM programs. He was responsible for the laboratory’s accreditation and for the methods now used on both lead-acid and 12V lithium-ion work.

He sits on industry standards discussions and remains the laboratory’s point of contact for test planning.

In memoriam

John Z.

Co-founder and Technical Director · 1989–2020

John helped build the Redford laboratory and its procedures from the first year. His work ran from flooded lead-acid through the early 12V lithium-ion programs. The laboratory still runs to the discipline he set: the method, then the data, then the report.

Accreditation

A2LA 1806.01

The laboratory is accredited by A2LA to ISO/IEC 17025:2017 in the electrical field of testing. Certificate 1806.01 is current through 30 September 2027. Contact of record: Mike Kaler.
