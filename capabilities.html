Batteries

What we accept

Lead-acid and 12V lithium-ion. The historic work, and the work now in scope.

Lead-acid

Automotive and industrial cells and batteries. This is the laboratory’s historic work.

  • SLI — starting, lighting, ignition
  • Deep cycle
  • AGM
  • Gel cell
  • Flooded lead-acid

12V lithium-ion

In-scope electrical testing for 12-volt lithium-ion systems.

  • Lithium iron phosphate (LiFePO4)
  • Lithium cobalt oxide (LCO)
  • Lithium manganese oxide (LMO)
  • 12V packs and modules

Electrical

Performance

Named methods first. Customer procedures when they fall inside the laboratory’s ranges and A2LA scope.

  • Cold cranking

    SAE J537 cranking performance at the specified temperature and load.

  • Reserve capacity

    SAE J537 reserve-capacity discharge for starting, lighting, and ignition batteries.

  • Charge acceptance

    SAE J537 charge-acceptance measurement on lead-acid storage batteries.

  • Life — automotive

    SAE J240 life test and SAE J2801 comprehensive life test for 12 V automotive storage batteries.

  • Life — heavy duty

    SAE J2185 life test for heavy-duty storage batteries.

  • Storage-battery procedures

    SAE J360 and customer OEM procedures, as specified.

  • JIS starter batteries

    JIS D5301 and D5302 for lead-acid starter and motorcycle batteries.

  • 12V lithium-ion

    Electrical performance on 12-volt lithium-ion systems, including LiFePO4, LCO, and LMO.

Analysis

Warranty and returns

Independent evaluation of dealer and OEM returns. The report is written for disposition, not for a brand.

  • Charge state

    As-received voltage and state of charge on the returned unit.

  • Capacity

    Measured capacity against the rating or the specified method.

  • Inspection

    Visual and teardown notes when the return requires them.

  • Cause

    A written account of what the battery did, and what that implies for the claim.

Laboratory

Mechanical and environmental

Offered against a named method when the job requires it. Confirm the exact procedure when you request a plan. The electrical field of accreditation is A2LA 1806.01.

Environmental

Clients

Who sends work

Automotive OEMs, Tier 1 system integrators, aftermarket and replacement suppliers, and dealers sending warranty returns from across the country.

Passenger and commercial vehicles, marine systems, and industrial backup. The method is the same: the specification, then the data, then the report.

Process

How a job moves

  1. Plan

    Battery type, method, quantity, timeline, and the form of the report.

  2. Receive

    Chain of custody, as-received inspection, and baseline measurements.

  3. Test

    Execution to the approved plan, with the quality checks required by ISO/IEC 17025.

  4. Report

    Results, interpretation where requested, and certification documents when they apply.
