Battery Testing Engineer

Full-time · Redford, MI

Responsibilities

  • Design and execute test protocols for lead-acid and lithium-ion systems
  • Operate environmental chambers and battery cyclers
  • Collect, analyze, and report data to ISO/IEC 17025
  • Write custom plans with OEM clients
  • Hold the laboratory procedures

Requirements

  • Bachelor’s degree in electrical or mechanical engineering, or a related field
  • Two or more years in battery testing or a laboratory
  • Working knowledge of SAE, IEC, and ISO battery methods
  • Care with data
