Work

What we run

Electrical performance and warranty analysis for automotive storage batteries. Method numbers first. Interpretation second.

  • Cold cranking

    SAE J537 cranking performance at specified temperature and load.

  • Reserve capacity

    SAE J537 reserve-capacity discharge for starting, lighting, and ignition batteries.

  • Charge acceptance

    SAE J537 charge-acceptance measurement on lead-acid storage batteries.

  • Life testing

    SAE J240, SAE J2801, and SAE J2185 life cycles for automotive and heavy-duty storage batteries.

  • 12V lithium-ion

    In-scope electrical testing for 12-volt lithium-ion systems, including LiFePO4, LCO, and LMO chemistries.

  • Warranty analysis

    Independent evaluation of dealer and OEM returns — charge state, capacity, and cause.
